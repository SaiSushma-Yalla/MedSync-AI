HYPERTENSION GUIDANCE

Topic: Blood pressure and hypertension

General principles:

1. Blood pressure should be interpreted using appropriate clinical context and repeated measurements when necessary.

2. Hypertension management should consider the patient's overall cardiovascular and medical risk.

3. Kidney function may be relevant when evaluating hypertension and its treatment.

4. Medication decisions should take into account the patient's current medications and other medical conditions.

5. Patients should discuss persistent or significant blood pressure changes with their healthcare professional.

6. This document is a general reference and is not a substitute for professional medical advice.
