DIABETES GUIDANCE

Topic: Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus

General principles:

1. Diabetes management should be individualized according to the patient's clinical condition.

2. Blood glucose and HbA1c should be monitored according to the patient's treatment plan.

3. Medication decisions should consider kidney function, other medical conditions, current medications, and the patient's overall clinical situation.

4. Patients should discuss significant changes in glucose measurements or diabetes treatment with their healthcare professional.

5. This document is a general reference and is not a substitute for professional medical advice.
