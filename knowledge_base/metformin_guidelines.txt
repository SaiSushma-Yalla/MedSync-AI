METFORMIN GUIDANCE

Topic: Metformin and kidney function

General principles:

1. Kidney function should be considered when evaluating the appropriateness and dosing of metformin.

2. Estimated glomerular filtration rate (eGFR) is an important measurement when evaluating metformin use.

3. Reduced kidney function may affect how metformin therapy should be managed.

4. Kidney function should be monitored according to appropriate clinical guidance.

5. Patients should not start, stop, increase, or decrease metformin without guidance from their healthcare professional.

6. This document is a general reference and is not a substitute for professional medical advice.
